One place for your church's members, giving, attendance and notices

Many Adventist churches still keep member lists in an exercise book, tithe records in a spreadsheet and announcements in a WhatsApp group. KanisaHub brings these together so the clerk, the treasurer and the elders all work from the same up-to-date record.

See what it does

Four jobs it takes off your desk

Member records

Names, families, baptism dates, departments and contacts, kept in one searchable register instead of several notebooks.

Tithe and offerings

Every tithe and offering is recorded against a fund, so the treasurer can report to the church board without re-adding envelopes.

Attendance

Mark who was at Sabbath School, Divine Service or a midweek meeting, and see who has not been seen for a while.

Notices and events

Announce programmes and reach members from the same system that holds their records.

Who it is for

KanisaHub is designed for local congregations that run on volunteers: a church clerk who keeps the register, a treasurer who counts offerings after service, and elders who follow up on members. It does not ask a church to buy special equipment. A phone or a shared computer is enough.
