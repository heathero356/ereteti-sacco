Events and programme

A typical Adventist church keeps a steady weekly rhythm with a few special services added through the year. KanisaHub holds the weekly programme in one place and lets leaders add one-off events on top of it.

Example weekly programme for a local church

How events are announced

When a leader adds an event, they choose who should hear about it: the whole church or a single department. The date and time then appear on the programme page and in the notice that members receive. If the time changes, the leader edits it once and everyone sees the new version.
