About KanisaHub

The problem we are solving

A local Adventist church holds a great deal of information: who belongs, who is in which department, what was given and where it went, who attended and who has gone quiet. In many congregations this lives in paper registers, personal phones and spreadsheets that only one person understands. When that person is away, or leaves the role, the record is hard to rebuild.

Paper records are also slow to total, easy to lose and difficult to check. A treasurer who must add up weeks of envelopes by hand has little time left to explain the figures to the church board.

What we are building

KanisaHub is a church management system covering four areas: member records and data management, tithe and offering tracking, attendance and engagement, and communication and event coordination. It is meant for congregations that currently depend on manual records, basic tools such as spreadsheets and WhatsApp, or existing software that leaves gaps for a small church.
