Tithes and offerings

Adventist congregations return tithe and several kinds of offering, and each one goes to a different place. KanisaHub records every gift against its fund at the moment it is counted, so the totals the treasurer reports always match the records.

What changes for the treasurer

Instead of copying envelope amounts into a spreadsheet after service, the treasurer enters each gift once. Monthly summaries by fund are produced from those entries, and a member can be given a statement of their own giving on request.

Privacy first

Only the treasurer enters and opens individual giving records. Pastors and the board see totals, and each member sees only their own statement.
