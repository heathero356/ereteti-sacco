Features

KanisaHub is organised around the four things a church office does every week. Each one works on its own, and all four share the same member register.

Member records

Keep one profile for every member: contact details, family links, baptism and membership dates, and the departments they serve in. Search by name, department or family, and update a record in seconds when someone moves or joins.

Replaces: the paper register and scattered contact lists.

Tithe and offering tracking

Record each tithe and offering against its fund when it is counted. Totals update as you go, and the treasurer can print a summary for the church board. Members can be given a statement of what they have returned.

Replaces: the offering book and end-of-month spreadsheet.

Attendance and engagement

Take attendance at Sabbath School, Divine Service and midweek meetings. Elders and deacons can see who has missed several weeks in a row and plan a visit before the gap grows.

Replaces: the attendance sheet passed along the pews.

Communication and events

Post the week's programme, announce a special service and send a notice to the whole church or to one department. Members see the same dates the leaders see, so nobody depends on a forwarded message.

Replaces: long WhatsApp threads where notices get buried.

Built for small teams

Most churches have two or three volunteers doing the admin. Every screen is meant to be learned in one sitting.
