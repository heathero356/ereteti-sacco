Who uses KanisaHub

A church office is a team of volunteers, and not everyone should see everything. KanisaHub gives each church officer the tools for their own work and hides the rest.

Roles in a local church and what they do in the system
Role Main work in KanisaHub Can see giving records?
Church clerk Keeps the member register, records transfers, baptisms and removals No
Treasurer Records tithes and offerings and prepares reports for the church board Yes, all funds
Elder Follows up on members who have stopped attending and reviews attendance trends No
Pastor Views summaries across membership, attendance and events Totals only
Department leader Manages their department list and posts its notices and events No
Member Checks their own details, the programme and notices Own statement only

Keeping giving records visible only to the people who need them protects members' privacy and protects the officers who handle the money.

Why separate roles?

A member's giving is personal. Limiting who can open it builds trust between the congregation and its treasurer.
